\# Recommendation: Urgency Badge Rollout — Quick Commerce Platform



\## Question

Does displaying a 'Only 3 left!' urgency badge on low-stock products

increase add-to-cart rate, and is the lift large enough to justify rollout?



\## Method

A/B test across 50,000 user sessions (25,000 control, 25,000 treatment)

over a simulated 14-day window. Primary metric: add-to-cart rate.

Statistical test: chi-square test of independence at α = 0.05.

Effect size measured using Cohen's h. Confidence interval calculated

at 95% using the normal approximation method.



\## Findings



\*\*Finding 1 — Statistically significant lift:\*\*

The urgency badge increased add-to-cart rate from \~18% to \~22%,

a 4+ percentage point lift (p < 0.001, 95% CI entirely above zero).

The result is not due to chance.



\*\*Finding 2 — Effect is strongest in Evening sessions:\*\*

Evening (5pm–9pm) shows the highest lift at 5.8pp vs 2.1pp in Morning.

Users in browsing mode respond more strongly to urgency cues than

users in task-completion mode.



\*\*Finding 3 — Lift is consistent across all product categories:\*\*

No category shows zero or negative lift, confirming the badge

works platform-wide rather than for specific product types only.



\*\*Finding 4 — Test is well-powered:\*\*

Actual sample size per group exceeds the minimum required for 80% power,

confirming the result is reliable and not an artifact of insufficient data.



\## Recommendation

Roll out the urgency badge to all product categories platform-wide,

prioritising Evening (5pm–9pm) and Night (9pm–12am) slots where lift

is highest. At Blinkit/Swiggy Instamart scale, this is projected to

generate approximately ₹57 crore in additional monthly revenue.

If a phased rollout is required, begin with Evening slots and

expand after 2 weeks of live validation.



\## Limitations

\- Synthetic data — validate with live traffic before full rollout

\- Novelty effect not controlled for — run for 4+ weeks in production

\- Add-to-cart rate is a leading metric; purchase completion not tracked

\- Brand trust and repeat purchase rate should be monitored post-rollout
